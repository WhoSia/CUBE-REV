## Prospective P6 claims

### P6-H1 — q is not two-phase-cost sufficient
The phase-1 coordinate `q` may determine exact phase-1 transitions and `d1` while failing to determine the downstream phase-2 cost fiber.

### P6-H2 — cost fiber can compress endpoint identity
Literal endpoint identity is stronger than necessary for scalar two-phase cost questions. States may be quotientable by their entry-cost signature even when their endpoint sets differ.

### P6-H3 — slack frontier is a distinct target
A representation sufficient for shortest-entry cost need not preserve the effect of allowing an extra phase-1 move.

### P6-H4 — cube symmetry should compress without changing cost signatures
For the executable U-D-axis C4 subgroup, cost-fiber and one-slack signatures should transport invariantly after action/state relabeling.

### P6-H5 — the mathematics should produce cognitive contrasts, not cognitive conclusions
Same-q / different-cost-fiber states are candidate matched stimuli for future human planning research. No machine representation is identified with a human cognitive representation without behavioral evidence.
