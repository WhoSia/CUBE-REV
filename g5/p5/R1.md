# G5-P5-R1 — Phase-Slack Geometry, Coset Entry-Surface Structure, Symmetry-Reduced Extremal States, Pattern-Database Lower Bounds, Geodesic Distortion & Representation-Transport Court

## Explanandum
R0 established nontrivial first-hit phase distortion. R1 asks **why** the distortion appears statewise.

For slack 0,
[
d_{2P}^{(0)}(s)=d_O(s)+min_{hin E_0(s)} d_H(h),
]
where (E_0(s)) is the set of orientation-solved entry states reachable along a shortest orientation-solving path.

Thus
[
Delta_{2P}^{(0)}(s)
=d_O(s)+min_{hin E_0(s)}d_H(h)-d_G(s).
]

R1 treats the entry surface, not merely the final scalar gap, as the explanatory object.

## Prospective exact coordinates
For every state:
- d_G: unrestricted anchored-RUF geodesic;
- d_O: exact orientation-coordinate distance;
- d_P: unrestricted permutation-coordinate distance;
- h_OP=max(d_O,d_P): admissible product-coordinate PDB lower bound;
- entry_H_min: minimum H2-restricted distance among shortest-orientation entries;
- entry_H_max: maximum H2-restricted distance among shortest-orientation entries;
- entry_width=entry_H_max-entry_H_min;
- Delta_0=d_O+entry_H_min-d_G.

The PDB authority is deliberately modest: h_OP is an exact quotient-coordinate lower bound, not a claim to reproduce Korf's cubie-subset PDB construction.

## Rival explanations
- H_ENTRY: distortion is largely an entry-surface phenomenon; short orientation paths funnel into H2-distant permutations.
- H_ORI: orientation depth alone largely determines distortion.
- H_PDB: generic quotient-search hardness h_OP largely determines distortion.
- H_SYM: distortion respects the full graph symmetry and is therefore intrinsic to the state rather than representation coordinates.
- H_REP: geodesic geometry is symmetric but the phase representation breaks that symmetry; distortion is representation-relative.

No regression/correlation scalar may be promoted to mechanism. Exact collision/counterexample structure has priority.
