# R1.15 Preliminary Literature-gap Bridge

> 상태: **targeted preliminary search**, systematic review 아님. Novelty authority는 HOLD.

## 검색에서 확인한 인접 연구군

### 1. Real-world skill acquisition

Meinz et al. (2023), *Ability and Nonability Predictors of Real-World Skill Acquisition: The Case of Rubik's Cube Solving*, Journal of Intelligence. Novices (N=79)를 7-step method로 훈련하고 fluid intelligence, working memory, personality/nonability variables와 skill acquisition을 연결한다.

- DOI: 10.3390/jintelligence11010018
- 역할: `skill acquisition / expertise development neighbor`
- CUBE-REV와의 차이: reconstruction trace의 construct-validity/observation-operator problem이 중심이 아니다.

### 2. Macro-operators and non-serializable subgoals

Korf (1985), *Macro-operators: A weak method for learning*, Artificial Intelligence 26(1):35–77.

Rubik's Cube처럼 non-serializable subgoal을 가진 문제에서 primitive operator sequence를 macro로 학습하는 문제를 다룬다.

- DOI: 10.1016/0004-3702(85)90012-8
- 역할: `algorithm/subgoal representation neighbor`
- CUBE-REV와의 차이: human reconstruction trace의 observation operator validation이 아니라 problem-solving/learning algorithm의 representation 문제다.

### 3. Decomposition and incomplete representation

*Decomposition Patterns in Problem Solving*은 simplified Rubik-like problem에서 decomposition strategy와 weak suboptimality, incomplete representation을 다룬다.

- 역할: `subproblem/decomposition cognition neighbor`
- CUBE-REV와의 차이: 실제 competition reconstruction trace와 frozen state/null/transport authority가 아니다.

### 4. Collective expertise and algorithm acquisition

Krakauer, Kardeş & Grochow (2025), *Universality in Collective Intelligence on the Rubik's Cube*는 competitive cube communities의 장기 performance와 algorithm acquisition을 collective expertise의 관점에서 분석한다.

- arXiv:2511.18609
- 역할: `competitive community / expert algorithm knowledge neighbor`
- CUBE-REV와의 차이: aggregate longitudinal expertise rather than move-level reconstruction-to-construct measurement validity.

### 5. Computational state/symmetry representations

Rubik's Cube solving-agent literature는 cube-state representation, whole-cube rotations, color transformations, symmetries, search/RL을 다룬다.

- 역할: `state representation / symmetry neighbor`
- CUBE-REV와의 차이: computational solver performance와 human-trace construct inference는 authority problem이 다르다.

## 현재 gap hypothesis

Targeted search에서 직접 analogue를 아직 찾지 못했다:

> **실제 expert reconstruction trace를 notation → cubie state → phase objective → route coordinate gauge → method ontology로 단계적으로 변환하면서, 각 observation operator의 representation invariance, null behavior, future/unseen transport, provenance, claim authority를 별도 court로 검증하는 접근.**

그러나 이 문장은 novelty claim이 아니라 **search hypothesis**다.
