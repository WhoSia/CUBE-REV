# AK4 — Parity character of the edge-color automorphism group

**Status:** mathematical generator proof and independent finite computational cross-check; the group-wide sign identity must still be added as a Lean theorem. **Focus:** remove a costly and opaque 12-label parity recalculation from every transported certificate leaf.

## Setup

Let `H=K_{2,2,2}` have opposite-pair blocks `P_1,P_2,P_3`. Each `g∈G=Aut(H)` induces two permutations:
- `τ_g∈S₃` of the three blocks;
- `α_g∈S₁₂` of the graph's twelve unordered edges (the physical edge cubie types).

**Theorem AK4 (edge-sign factorization).**

```text
sgn(α_g) = sgn(τ_g)                    for every g∈G.
```

Equivalently, `parity(α_g)=parity(τ_g)` in `ℤ/2ℤ`.

## Structural proof using group generators

The automorphism group is generated by swaps of the two colors inside one opposite pair and swaps of two entire opposite pairs.

**Within-pair generator:** swap `U↔D`, fixing the other four colors. An edge incident to `U` is exchanged with the corresponding edge incident to `D`: four disjoint transpositions on the twelve edge types. All other edges are fixed. Hence the induced edge permutation is even; the induced pair permutation is the identity, also even. The same holds for the other two within-pair generators.

**Pair-transposition generator:** swap `P_1={U,D}` with `P_2={R,L}` using a fixed matching of the two internal color positions. The four edges between these two blocks are mapped by transposing endpoints; this yields **one** transposition of the two off-diagonal edges, while the two diagonal edges are fixed. The four edges from `P_1` to the third block are interchanged in four disjoint transpositions with the four from `P_2` to the third block. Therefore the induced edge permutation has five transpositions in total and is **odd**. The pair permutation is one transposition, also odd. The same argument works for every pair swap.

Both `sgn∘α` and `sgn∘τ` are homomorphisms `G→{±1}`, and they agree on a generating set; hence they agree on all of `G`. QED.

This is a group-character computation, not an exhaustive check over `12!` edge permutations. The independent `audit_r029_hall_burnside_graph_quotient.py` additionally checked the identity on all 48 elements, using independent inversion counts on the induced 12-edge and 3-pair maps.

## Composition with position sorting

Represent the original complete 12-edge permutation by `π`, where `π(i)` is the original edge type in physical edge position `i`. Let `β` be the permutation taking a canonical representative index to the original position index, and let `α_g` be the chosen color-induced edge-label permutation. The normalized arrangement is

```text
π_rep = α_g ∘ π ∘ β.
```

Since permutation sign is multiplicative,

```text
parity(π_rep)
  = parity(π) XOR parity(β) XOR parity(τ_g).
```

**Conventions matter:** the exact `β` direction must match the code's `order` array, and sign of the inverse equals sign of the permutation; thus the parity correction is robust to using `β^{-1}` only at the sign level, not to accidentally changing the actual address pullback.

For the first eleven colors `c_0,…,c_10`, `β` is the stable sorting permutation by ranks of `g(c_i)`. The sign of this stable sorting permutation equals the parity of the number of strictly inverted pairs,

```text
Σ_{0≤i<j<11} [rank(g(c_i)) > rank(g(c_j))]  (mod 2).
```

Equal-color elements are never swapped by stable sorting. This yields a closed, history-only correction using at most 55 pairwise comparisons and the parity of a 3-element block permutation. It does **not** depend on any unknown second edge color.

## Why this matters for frame 19

The actual corner scan, not the color relabeling itself, determines the physical corner cubie permutation sign. The legal Rubik cube requires edge permutation sign = corner permutation sign. Under normalization, the leaf decoder must use

```text
edge_sign_rep = corner_sign_original XOR parity(β) XOR parity(τ_g).
```

Only after this correction can a pair of opposite-sign normalized edge candidates be disambiguated by the terminal corner data. A leaf with two opposite *normalized* signs is not automatically a singleton under the *uncorrected* physical corner sign.
