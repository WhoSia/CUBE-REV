## Established mathematical picture

- The **primary theorem** is the 18-frame impossibility, already represented by `PhysicalEighteen.physical_eighteen_impossible` in the historical pinned Lean build. This theorem permits free legal face turns chosen adaptively before both colors, uses a fixed edge/corner probe, and produces two distinct reachable initial cubes with solved corners having identical transcripts.
- The **matching 19-frame policy** reads one sticker of eleven edges and schedules the first eleven corner observations, then spends eight more frames on the frozen 52-representative adaptive edge trees while obtaining three additional *new* corner colors. Every frame has exactly one edge and one corner address; the final unique corner reading is at frame 19. The last read is **not** available when the controller chooses that frame's edge address.
- Previous W checked the 52 trees, the 1,362 feasible count vectors and the coordinate transport bijection across 3,215,616 normalized edge records separately. X now **runs the entire abstract controller** for each of those records, decodes the unique terminal word *after* applying the corrected corner parity, and inverts the canonicalization. All records match.
- X also implements a *nineteen-frame physical controller* with real 48-sticker face-quarter-turn permutations, fixed probes `DL[1]` and `DLB[1]`, route table length 576, and explicit observations of fourteen corner sticker addresses. It reconstructed all 48 moving stickers for 1,000 reproducible legal scrambles generated by quarter turns.
- A bounded physical pose compiler records the **canonical setup** relative to the initial pose, rather than recursively embedding the full prior turn history. For fixed probes `DL[1]`, `DLB[1]`, each base setup uses at most 9 quarter turns and the inter-frame move word has at most **18** (lookup-order) quarter turns. The observation budget remains 19; free-turn count is not optimized.

## Still open before claiming a completely Lean-verified 19-frame optimum

1. **Physical legality to corner coordinates:** prove that every reachable `Coloring` admits `CornerCoordinates` whose piece labels are a permutation, whose twist sum vanishes modulo 3, and whose two ordered colors agree with physical sticker reads. Generator-level invariants have been independently audited but not yet linked to the `Reachable` predicate in Lean.
2. **Universal first-eleven transport:** in Lean, connect every first-color record, not just its sorted count vector, via the 48 legal color relabelings and the 11-position stable sorting to its representative `sector` list, preserving query responses and the exact permutation sign correction.
3. **Frozen finite tree to executable abstract policy:** turn the checked 52 representative trees into a single 19-read `Policy (Fin 24 × Fin 24) FrameColor Coloring` whose output equals the initial coloring. Show corner schedule, terminal sign selection and inverse transport are total on all reachable inputs; apply the physical compiler theorem to obtain the final theorem.
4. **CI trust audit:** obtain a successful pinned Lean build and exact `#print axioms` for the bridge and the final theorem. `native_decide` remains a genuine evaluator trust dependency, which cannot be quietly discarded.
5. **Secondary fixed optimum:** the preset 21-frame result is a mathematically distinct claim that still needs a formal counterpart if the submission headline describes both optima as kernel-verified.
