## Reason for transition, learned from P3-R2/R3

We preserved valuable finite observations and coding-theoretic boundaries, but studying more variations of abstract observation symbols, documentation protocols or human mental labels will no longer be the main research programme. The research target has always been the actual legal 3×3 cube, its original source states, whole-cubie constraints and executable search mechanisms. TCS is essential **as the theory of cube state spaces, subgroup geometry, exact shortest move words, algorithm description/execution costs, heuristics, lower bounds and proof certificates**, rather than a free-standing philosophy of observation.

The current P3-R3B result makes this pivot concrete. The deliberately selected 62-state same-corner fiber is extremely special: all 62 original real 54-sticker states are within five six-generator halfturn moves; every global all18 shortest word uses **only** those half turns; 50 have exactly one geodesic word and 12 exactly two, totaling 74. A common original-cube 3+2 meet-in-middle solver uses a goal radius2 **34-state** frontier and source radius3 **154-state** search to produce 62 distinct globally shortest solutions, one per original state. This is a finite exact physical theorem and a working algorithmic design, **not** a new generic Rubik solver, empirical cognition result or bit-size compression minimum. Out-of-domain single quarter-turn controls are explicitly tested rather than concealed.

The immediately inherited independent human FMC primary-source states from CUBE-REV 0.22/0.23 are fundamentally different ecologies. Their main-stage original authored words, group axes, NISS and published final-word representations require source-aware parsing. Matching human written candidates is not observing a complete psychological consideration set. The 451 private reconstruction corpus remains source-private, retrospective, and without final strict balanced support.

## P1 — Geodesic structure and subgroup-aware search synthesis

Treat mathematical subgroup knowledge as prior art; a scientific contribution must be a rigorously new mechanism, counterexample, or measured improvement.

## P2 — Counterfactual physical solver invention and falsification
