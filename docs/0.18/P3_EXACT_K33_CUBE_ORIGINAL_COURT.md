## Locked cube problem, not an unrelated automata example

Let the single tracked UR-edge start at any one of twelve possible edge positions with its orientation bit fixed at zero. Legal physical moves are the 18 standard 3×3×3 HTM face turns, implemented by the original sticker-cube oracle and reflected into the 24-state tracked-edge action table. A four-move word `w=(a1,a2,a3,a4)` produces a **four-bit intrinsic orientation observation history** `h_w(p)∈{0,1}^4` after each action for every initial slot `p∈{0,..,11}`.

The frozen admissible source family `S` has **1,192 subsets** of the initial twelve slots, selected by the original cube-specific 3/4/5-slot family rule. An experiment `w` resolves an admissible source set `S∈𝓢` iff `h_w|S` is injective. Define the distinguished-source coverage set `C(w)={S∈𝓢 : h_w|S injective}`. A fixed experiment **dictionary** `W` is universally adequate iff `⋃_{w∈W}C(w)=𝓢`. The research target is `M* = min{|W|: W is adequate}`. The choice of experiment may depend on the source subset, **not** on feedback during the experiment. This is not the number of moves necessary to solve a Rubik's Cube, nor a full 3×3×3 state identification theorem or a finding about human cognition.

### Proven finite reduction (pure combinatorial lemma)

Every length-four legal action word is from `18^4=104,976` literal words. The exact physical-oracle exporter yields **1,913** history partitions, **1,477** distinct coverage subsets, and a maximum-inclusion antichain of **1,323** coverage sets. For each dominated coverage set `A⊂B`, replacing a word covering `A` by a word covering `B` cannot reduce the union. Therefore an adequate dictionary of at most `k` words exists iff such a dictionary exists among the **1,323 maximal physical representatives**. If replacements lead to duplicate words, the number can only decrease. This lemma holds for every `k`, unlike the earlier `168` near-tight class restriction that was legitimate only for the historical k24 argument.

### Exact original CNF and proof-checking contract

Let `x_j` represent using maximal physical dictionary word `j∈{1,...,1323}`. For each original admissible source set `S_i∈𝓢`, add a clause `∨_{j:S_i∈C(w_j)}x_j`. Add an **unweighted at-most-k** cardinality encoding with fresh auxiliary variables and no other domain restrictions. This is equisatisfiable with `M*≤k` by the covering lemma; SAT must be turned into actual four-move word representatives and replayed on the sticker-derived physical cube transitions, and UNSAT must be accompanied by the byte-exact original CNF, full trace and independent DRUP/DRAT verification. DRUP proof validity by itself does not prove that the Rubik action table was modeled correctly; the physical-to-incidence generation is an additional separate proof obligation.

## Research-paper threshold and novelty limits

A theoretically sound paper should contain (i) the cube experiment contract in unambiguous face-action and flip-observation coordinates, (ii) honest connection to existing FSM distinguishing/homing sequences and fixed dictionary/set-cover optimization, (iii) the all-k 1,323 maximal-mask theorem, (iv) full byte-indexed physical source→CNF reproducibility and semantic audit demonstrating why historical P6 failed, (v) independently checked UNSAT proof and independently physical-replayed matching upper witness, and (vi) scope-limited implications for actual 3×3×3 edge-history inference, with no unsupported extension to all cubies or human cognition.

**Scientific honesty:** exact M* would strengthen the paper; however novelty is NOT established simply by a hard finite optimum, nor by mechanically invoking classical automata concepts. Peer-review worthiness depends on whether the cube-specific structural reduction, failure-of-model-transfer analysis, and proof-carrying reproducible reconstruction identify a nontrivial generalizable insight beyond this particular 1,192-row instance.
