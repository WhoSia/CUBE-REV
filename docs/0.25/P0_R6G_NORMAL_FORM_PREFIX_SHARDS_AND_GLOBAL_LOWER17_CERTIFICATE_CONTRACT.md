## Formal regular-language partition

Let A={0,...,17} be the 18 distinct original canonical HTM symbols in fixed URFDLB/power order; L≤R be the regular language accepted by the actual source normal-form DFA with budget R. Every normal-form word belongs to EXACTLY one of 19 disjoint classes:
- The empty word, tested separately (repeated once in each of the R+1 iterative depth limits).
- For each exact first generator a∈A, the suffix language {a w : a w∈L≤R}. The 18 classes are mutually disjoint because any nonempty word has one and only one first symbol. Each class carries the actual source physical state reached by that first generator and the DFA last-face context a.

Define c_{a,d} as the number of canonical words of total length d≥1 beginning with a. Its exact iterative source call budget under depth limits 0..R is

    N_R(a)=sum_{d=1..R} (R-d+1) c_{a,d}.

Hence the exact disjoint complete-cover identity is

    N_R=(R+1)+sum_{a∈A}N_R(a).

This counts every repeated recursive visit in the historical original R6E iterative-search proof contract, not physical distinct states. A future shard solver MAY instead enumerate unique prefixes once, but then it must declare and independently audit a different appropriate count invariant, rather than incorrectly compare against the iterative budget.

The original source DFA face normal form collapses, for counting purposes, to classes of low first face {U,R,F} (9 actual HTM actions) and high first face {D,L,B} (9 actions). Its matrix T=[[6,6],[9,6]] yields the exact B10 shard totals:

- Exact source proof identity: 11 + 9×17,489,205,559 + 9×14,279,876,542 = **285,921,738,920**.
