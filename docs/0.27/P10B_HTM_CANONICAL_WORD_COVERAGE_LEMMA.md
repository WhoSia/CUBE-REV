# CUBE-REV 0.27 P10b — Elementary HTM Canonical-Word Coverage Lemma

### Claim (unconditional within frozen 18HTM face-turn semantics)
For each legal HTM solution word \(w\), there exists a word \(N(w)\) in the grammar excluding (a) two adjacent moves on the same face and (b) consecutive opposite-axis faces in reversed fixed order, such that \(|N(w)|\le |w|\) and both words act by the **same physical 54-site permutation**. In the frozen indexing [U,R,F,D,L,B], the forbidden opposite ordered adjacency is (D,U), (L,R), (B,F); it is the exact predicate `last_face - next_face == 3` used by RubikOptimal's `solver.search`. This lemma removes a legitimate *word-grammar coverage* assumption; it does not validate arbitrary pruning heuristics, source goal states, or global no17 completeness.

### Short direct proof
Same-face consecutive turns are rotations by \(90,180,270\) degrees of one face. Sum exponents modulo four: two adjacent same-face moves reduce to one HTM turn, or disappear. Moves on opposite faces of the same axis commute exactly as elements of the physical cube group, so reverse-ordered adjacent opposite-face moves may swap without changing length. Apply either local rewriting rule until no forbidden adjacency remains. Length never increases. Any shortening strictly decreases length; at fixed length, a reversed opposite-face swap strictly decreases the finite lexicographic face-index word, so repeated rewriting terminates. Each rewrite preserves the exact cube permutation; therefore normal representatives exist at no higher HTM length.
