# CUBE-REV — The Radius-Four Information Barrier and Exact Mixed-Sensor Minimum
30 October 2026 · Full 54-sticker 3×3 cube · Exact, bounded mathematics

## 1. Domain, assumptions, and provenance

The group G of legal fixed-center Rubik's Cube states is generated by the 18 outer-face HTM actions. Its Cayley distance d is the minimum number of HTM moves. B_r is the solved-state ball of radius r. A tagged original edge has 12 physical positions and two orientations. An original corner has 8 positions and three orientations.

The idealized edge sensor reads only a binary orientation of its particular original cubie after legal actions. The idealized corner sensor reads the ternary location of its U/D-colored reference sticker within the current three facelet slots. Neither sensor directly supplies the tagged cubie's position; the position+orientation is a hidden CLOSED physical factor. All measurements are noiseless and the policy may use arbitrary feedback turns. The target is the unique full 54-sticker SOLVED state.

The 24-state factor automata have their own distinguishing property: every distinct tagged-edge pair separates with at most two further turns; every distinct tagged-corner pair separates with an immediate reading or one further turn. All eight corner automata were checked on all 276 factor pairs each: 192 immediately separate and 84 after at most one move.

All reported cube calculations use the original CUBE-REV 0.24 spatial 54-sticker 18HTM semantics, with a separately written Python representation and independent source-faithful Node check. The 0.27 P22 shallow physical court was separately replayed below; the original deep P8/P11 source/B6 C++ executable was NOT acquired or run.

## 2. Theorem: factor injectivity radius equals half the shortest kernel element

Let pi_E(s) be the vector of position+orientation factors of the selected tagged cubies. Let K_E be the subgroup fixing all those tagged cubies at solved state. Let ell(K_E) = min{|g|_HTM : g in K_E and g != identity}, with ell = infinity when K_E is trivial.

The projection pi_E is injective on the entire ball B_r if and only if:

K_E intersect B_(2r) = {identity}.

Proof. Two distinct source configurations x,y in B_r have identical pi_E precisely if y^(-1)x is a nonidentity element of K_E. Its length is at most 2r. Conversely every g in K_E with length at most 2r can be split at its shortest-word midpoint as g = u v, |u|<=r and |v|<=r. Then the two configurations represented by u^(-1) and v belong to B_r, have the same physical tagged factors, and differ. QED.

Consequently the maximum radius of complete full-goal contingent solvability under distinguishable tagged factors is:

r_max(E) = floor((ell(K_E)-1)/2),

provided the factor outputs can themselves be separated by finite action experiments. The above finite 24-state edge/corner output checks establish this premise for the selected sensor family.

This is an algebraic specialization of classical stabilizer/quotient geometry and automata observability, NOT an assertion that the generic theorem is academically novel.

## 3. Exact eight-HTM edge-kernel girth

Choose ALL twelve original edge tags, each with full hidden position+orientation factor. Exact breadth-first physical solved-state balls have shell counts:

radius 0..4: 1, 18, 243, 3240, 43239
total B3: 3502; total B4: 46741.

All 3502 edge-factor projections on B3 are distinct. All edge factors in the new depth-four shell are distinct from every edge factor in B3. Consequently there cannot be a nonidentity all-edge-fixing word with length <=7: otherwise split its word into <=3 and <=4 moves and obtain a forbidden B3/B4 collision.

On the other hand, the explicitly physically checked eight-HTM word

U L U' R U L' U' R'

is a nontrivial three-corner permutation fixing all twelve edges at their exact original positions and orientations. It is a commutator of U L U' with R. Hence:

ell(K_all_12_edges) = 8; r_max(all-edges-only) = 3.

This explains the sudden failure of all-edge-only sensor policies at radius4. No additional number of *edge-only* readings can distinguish the hidden full cube states.

Among the 46741 full physical B4 states, the complete 12-edge-factor map has exactly 46309 distinct images. There are 432 collision classes of size exactly two; both members in every pair have exact full-cube distance4. A concrete class is:

A = U L U' R
B = R U L U'

These are different complete 54-sticker cubes with the same complete original twelve-edge factors. An independently implemented Node physical sticker transition check reproduced this exact pair and the eight-HTM commutator, including a nonzero difference of 7 face stickers.

The existence of 8-move three-corner commutators is known in Rubik's Cube literature, for example Commutators in the Rubik's Cube Group, American Mathematical Monthly (2023). Here the additional finite radius and kernel-girth proof is scoped to the project's precisely defined sensors.

## 4. Exact minimum mixed edge+corner tags for B4: 14

All 432 all-edge-factor collision pairs differ in exactly three tagged corners. Across the pairs, ALL 56 possible 3-of-8 sets occur as changed-corner supports. Thus any successful sensor set must hit every 3-set of corners; equivalently at most two corner tags may remain unobserved. At least SIX tagged corners are necessary regardless of how many tagged edges are selected.

Independently, group all B4 sources by their full eight-corner-factor vector. The pairs agreeing on all eight corners supply 1113 distinct nonempty changed-edge supports. For every one of the C(12,7)=792 seven-edge choices, there is a recorded physical collision pair whose support misses that seven-edge choice; therefore any successful design needs at least EIGHT tagged edges regardless of how many corner sensors are selected.

A complete check of the C(12,8)=495 eight-edge subsets finds 102 whose edge support covers every all-eight-corner ambiguity. Crucially for this radius4 family, selecting ANY six of the eight corners leaves exactly the same 1113 residual edge-support constraints as selecting all eight corners: physical full-B4 pairs agreeing on six corner factors automatically agree on all eight.

Therefore a six-corner set combined with any of those 102 edge octets is injective on all 46741 complete physical B4 states. Exactly C(8,6)*102 = 28*102 = 2856 minimum designs work.

The result is exact:

minimum corners = 6
minimum edges = 8
minimum total tagged edge+corner sensors = 14
number of exact minimum designs = 2856.

One positive example:
- Original corners BDL, DFL, BLU, FLU, BDR, DFR.
- Original edges UR, UF, UL, UB, DR, DF, FR, BL.

This is a finite computational theorem with 56 plus 792 explicit lower-bound physical collision certificates and full-state injectivity checks for the positive designs. A separate receipt file provides all witness word pairs. All numbers apply to the idealized tagged-edge/tagged-corner sensing semantics, not arbitrary camera placement or a real robot sensor bill.
