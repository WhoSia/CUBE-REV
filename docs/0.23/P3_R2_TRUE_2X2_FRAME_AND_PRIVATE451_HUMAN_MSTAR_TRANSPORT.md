## 4. Why real archival action disagreement does not identify a human M* / neural cost

The two geometric/cognitive-inference maps are NOT interchangeable:

- **P3-R1 physical finite task:** one of 62 actual 3×3 physical states all sharing CP/CO, uniform synthetic prior; action library counts whole exact ≤5-HTM SOLVING WORDS; ideal observation operator reads actual complete nine colored stickers of a fixed cube face with zero time/recognition error; q2/q3 adaptive 42/58 vs fixed 38/50.
- **P3-R2B secondary human archive:** 451 selected written 3×3 speedsolve reconstructions, nonuniform unknown selection, mixture of face/slice/wide/rotation, long solve trajectories, retrospective source annotations and orientation proxies; the evaluated *observation* is full CP/CO perfect cubie metadata, NOT what people actually visually recognized. A next recorded MOVE is not a complete 5-HTM exact solving word.
- **2×2 record:** one real competition outcome + retrospective printed solve with one conditional unreported `y2` frame compatible under physical replay; no observed process traces, no measured gaze or information calls.

Thus the physically proven `M^ast(R;A,O,L,Π,B,C)` opportunity difference is **not estimable as a person's memory, inspection, heuristic chunk size, working memory, or neural activation difference from these data alone**. Do not transform the archive delta to a behavioral economist's latent System1/System2 label. Any legitimate experiment would need source-consistent per-move timestamps/inspection observations and proper participant/source blocking, and its own task/observation operator measured or logged.
