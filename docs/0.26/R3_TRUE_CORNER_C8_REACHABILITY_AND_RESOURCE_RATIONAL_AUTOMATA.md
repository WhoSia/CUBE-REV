## Why R3

R2 true original matched A/B 8-run physical court established that simply reordering the necessary corner/edge predicates conserves every visited and skipped word but is 13.35%/13.22% SLOWER. Rather than changing predicate order again, R3 constructs the exact inverse-reaching corner-state projection C8 from independently hash-pinned original C7 and actual 18 legal cubie CP/CO turns. A state failing C8 with remaining source depth2 provably cannot enter original full solved B6 in the remaining two physical moves. This can omit source subtrees *before* enumerating their legal pairs. The stronger predicate is standard pattern database theory (Korf 1997), not by itself a novel admissibility theorem.

Let Ck=pi_c(Bk). The physical corner projection is a deterministic transition homomorphism for every 18HTM action, so C8 = C7 union (union of images of C7 under all legal turns). For any whole cubie x, if pi_c(x) not in C8 and remaining depth<=2, there is no physical full state y in B6 reachable from x in at most2 steps. This holds even when the ordinary 18HTM word DFA restricts the legal future, because C8 is computed over a SUPERSET of allowable paths; the filter is still safe. This proof does not infer the analogous condition from corner position alone without twist, does not claim that C8 membership is sufficient for whole-cube solution and does not lift fixed-source physical 24-rotation orbit properties.

## RITHM link is structural rather than an untested claim about human psychology

[RITHM-2.7 P29 real finite-memory congestion automata](https://github.com/WhoSia/RITHM/blob/main/docs/rithm27_p29_finite_memory_congestion_automata_20261013.md) defined finite states, transition legality, information partitions, bounded memory, adversarial uncertainty, explicit mean cost and HOLD on unobserved human compliance. Reuse those **methodological constraints** here without claiming a measured link between human cognition and Rubik solver behavior.

- Physical transition system: legal complete CP/CO/EP/EO state x.
- Source-word automaton: last face/turn q in {START,0,...,17}, remaining depth r, canonical legal action relation delta(q,a).
- Proof state automaton: UNCHECKED → RUNNING → {CERTIFIED, TRUE_MEET, RESOURCE_HOLD, INVALID_RECEIPT}. Only original physical full-source finite search+independent complete audit may promote CERTIFIED; incomplete partial child does not promote an entire first-root.
- Observation authority: scheduling policy sees only pre-registered grammar size, actual completed receipts, C8 build cost, time/RSS and source clock; it cannot treat latent visits, unexecuted branches or absent human annotations as known.
- Weighted certificate task size W(q,r): independently count exactly all legal repeated IDDFS word calls. Expected time can be estimated from prior comparable completed tasks but is not a verified law. Maximize robust newly VERIFIED certificate coverage per runner-second and minimize regret from invalid expensive builds; preserve a fixed budget quota for high-value unusual branches so greed does not starve them.
- Early stop control: T0 trial reaches HOLD if guard construction too large, source run hits, independent physical/Go transcript disagrees, memory exceeded, or receipt missing. No false CLOSED stamp.

Russell and Wefald (1991), *Principles of Metareasoning*, DOI 10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C and Lieder & Griffiths (2019), *Resource-rational analysis*, are existing foundations for computation selection and resource-sensitive cognition. No originality claim for generic resource rationality, canonical DFA, or C8 projection; the possible research contribution is their correct verifiable integration on **a pinned original source with computational reproducibility and anti-forgery checks**.
