### PAPER B — Exact color-only two-probe full-cube identification (strong independent theorem)
Separate mathematical model: one stationary edge facelet probe + one stationary corner facelet probe, each returns one of six colors; arbitrary known legal 18-HTM action words and full legal state set.

Thus `h_nonadaptive=21`. A fixed complete-corner oracle gives `12!*2^10>6^15` and `h_adaptive>=16`; the 21-frame fixed construction gives `h_adaptive<=21`. Current achieved no-reset physical route 119 HTM for a fixed 21-frame construction, NOT HTM-optimal.
**Prior art:** active identifying/distinguishing sequences for FSMs (Lee and Yannakakis 1994; Hierons et al. 2008, newer ADS literature), adaptive feature acquisition and POMDP literature, existing 2017 MIT two-RGB-probe robot and camera scanning literature, ICLR 2026 CubeBench. A generic claim that two sensors can scan a cube, or that adaptivity helps, is not novel.
**Critical gate:** establish whether the 21 exact *nonadaptive* minimum is already known under an equivalent categorical facelet model; seek an adaptive exact horizon or strong adversarial lower bound beyond 16. Give 24/22/21-frame constructive proof with independently checked decoder and clearly defined sensor channel; distinguish categorical facelet probe from physical RGB camera.
**Verdict:** strong separate mathematical manuscript candidate, more substantial if adaptive 16–21 gap is closed or generalized to puzzle group families.

## Direct comparison / anti-overlap
- Classical **base**, graph **metric dimension**, short-radius **cubie base**, fixed-slot *full-identity* observation, fixed-slot **6-color** observation and image/RGB scanning are different observation functions. State equivalence precisely; none entails another paper's claimed optimum without a proof bridge.
- Static full original-labelled piece sensors cannot acquire fundamentally new information through deterministic known turns, because those selected piece labels' future locations follow from their already known states. Fixed *spatial* facelet probes may acquire new information as different pieces enter the slots. This epistemic-model distinction is central to honest PAPER A versus PAPER B scope.
- Infinite abstract group/action lemmas and existing Pareto or hitting-set methodology are not automatically new merely because they are expressed using Rubik terminology.
- Production GitHub CI PASS, independent source physical replay, raw source custody and mathematical proof are distinct authority levels.
