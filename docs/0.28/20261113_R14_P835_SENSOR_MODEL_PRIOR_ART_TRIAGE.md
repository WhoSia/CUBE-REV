## 1. Highest proximity: monocular cube recognition (2022 ICSPS / indexed 2023)

*Research on State Recognition of Rubik's Cube Based on Monocular Vision*, in the 2022 14th International Conference on Signal Processing Systems (ICSPS), DOI [10.1109/ICSPS58776.2022.00141](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSPS58776.2022.00141), publisher [IEEE Xplore document 10218571](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10218571/). The publisher abstract describes monocular video/camera image capture, color classification by k-nearest neighbors, geometric outline/pose information, six-sensor smart cube comparison, and AR-tag control guidance. **This is prior art for low-device-count cube state sensing**; no CUBE-REV author should claim that using one or two devices to identify a cube is itself novel.

**Precise scope contrast:** Regime B C0.3 yields exactly one categorical facelet color on one spatial edge and one spatial corner per frame. No image, outline, AR marker, object tracking, pose estimation or color uncertainty is provided. Its cost is the number of *paired site-query frames*, not the number of cameras, video frames or sensor devices. Hence the IEEE abstract does NOT by itself establish the same 21 fixed / 20 constructive adaptive exact mathematical horizon, and CUBE-REV does not refute or dominate that vision device.

## 3. Older cube one-hot correspondence optimization (2007): representational overlap, objective differs

S. R. Harker, C. R. Vogel and T. Gedeon (2007 manuscript), *Analysis of Constrained Optimization Variants of the Map-Seeking Circuit Algorithm*. CiteseerX accessible manuscript search contains a Rubik solver case with 24 states per original edge/corner cubie and one-hot representations. These 24-valued original-cubie factors are **not a new notation invented by CUBE-REV**; per original piece, 12 edge positions × 2 flips or 8 corners × 3 twists yield 24 poses. The 2007 paper concerns image correspondence/continuous relaxations for discrete transformation composition, not a classified minimum subset of persistently tagged pieces required for exact zero-error identification.

**Bibliographic limit:** PDF text metadata was located, but the first-page screenshot retrieval failed, and a full original PDF is not verified in Drive. Treat as a background candidate, NOT fully close-read prior work.
