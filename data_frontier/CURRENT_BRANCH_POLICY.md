# Current research branch policy

The active CUBE-REV research lineage must live on exactly one durable research branch: `research/current`.

`main` is reserved for the deployed/public surface. Historical experiment branches are not archival objects; important freezes are identified by commit SHA/tag and historical context is preserved in Notion, Google Drive, and the sealed pre-P14 full-history bundle.

Stage transitions should update the same active branch and remove superseded live-tree artifacts when they are no longer needed for reproduction or interpretation.
